This chapter is the shared vocabulary for the report and **assumes no background at all**. Each
entry gives two things: an explanation a non-specialist can follow, and why the term matters to the
argument made here — the part a general dictionary will not tell you.

Every chapter repeats the terms it needs at its own start, so you do not have to read this one
first. This is the complete version, for looking things up.

The small square on the left marks which family a term belongs to, with the same colours used
throughout:

- **Hardware root of trust** — depends on particular silicon, the costliest to replace, and the most concentrated in a few vendors' hands
- **Cryptography** — mathematical guarantees that need no trusted hardware, paid for in compute
- **Protocol and governance** — neither silicon nor mathematics, but agreement to follow the same rules
