This chapter is the report's self-audit. It does two jobs: hand anyone who wants to refute this a
ready-made list, and put "what we do not know" in plain sight rather than leaving it to be
discovered.

## Falsifiers for every record

The table below is generated from `data/claims/` and **is not maintained by hand**, so it cannot fall
behind what the chapters assert. Adding a record without a falsifier fails the build outright.

The tags are defined in [chapter 00](/agent-trust-stack/en/00-method/): `measured` means this report
ran it, `reproducible` means a formula or script is provided, `literature` means someone else's
published figure, and `inference` means this report's judgement. Entries marked "source owed" are
withheld from the `claims.json` export.

## The three refutations worth doing most

Every entry above is open to challenge, but three carry the most information — each can overturn a
whole chapter:

1. **Look up the ERC-8004 Validation Registry.** One block-explorer query. If it is deployed and
   genuinely used, section 7.1 of [chapter 07](/agent-trust-stack/en/07-cold-water/) is void.
2. **Check whether Apple PCC's attestation chain on Google Cloud can be verified by a third party.**
   Section 2.6 of [chapter 02](/agent-trust-stack/en/02-demand/) rests entirely on that unverified
   premise.
3. **Re-run the zkML proving benchmark.** If 1,000 tokens of a 7B model prove in minutes, the
   "eight days" extrapolation in section 3.2 of [chapter 03](/agent-trust-stack/en/03-cost/) and
   everything derived from it must be rewritten.

## What this report currently cannot do

Stated plainly:

1. **No measurements of its own.** Every figure is relayed from elsewhere. Nothing has been run on
   any specific machine, which is why no entry above is tagged `measured`.
2. **The Chinese and APAC ecosystem is entirely uncovered.** SecretFlow, FATE, the TEEs on Ascend and
   Kunpeng, Alibaba's confidential computing instances — none of it researched. The largest
   geographic blind spot, and the main intended differentiator.
3. **No pricing data.** "GPU TEE only costs 5–10% more" is **throughput loss**, not **list price**.
   What a confidential instance actually costs, and whether it forces a more expensive machine type,
   is not publicly quoted anywhere found. For judging commercial viability that matters more than the
   performance figure.
4. **Some figures still owe a primary source.** Flagged individually on the page and held out of the
   machine interfaces.
5. **The ERC-8004 data lags by four months.** The study window ends 2026-05; this snapshot is
   2026-09.
6. **Market-size figures cannot be cross-checked.** The houses differ by an order of magnitude, so
   this report can only show the divergence.
7. **Two adjacent threads are uncovered**: model weight exfiltration, and real enterprise deployment
   volume for federated learning.
